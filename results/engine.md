# The list-replay engine

Generated by `scripts/engine_check.py`: the pack, the starter kit's scripts and `gpr/engine.py` only. No LLM, nothing trained, and the locked test is not touched.

Skill is Pacific's pooled skill against the last value. 'Less error than drift' is 1 − pinball(engine) / pinball(drift) on the same cells, with a 90% interval from resampling (series, origin) blocks. 'Inside' is the share of truths in [q10, q90] (target 0.80).

## Offer lists

592 posted rental series have an offer list. The median over a day's offers reproduces the official value on 100.00% of series-days (592 of 592 series on every day).

## Shuffled-data control (validation origins)

Each shuffled copy keeps every offer and its listing pattern, and applies the real day-to-day price changes in a scrambled order. A change that lands on a day its offer is not listed is dropped, so a copy holds about four in five of the real changes. Baselines, engine and truth are all recomputed inside the copy. This is a rough control.
